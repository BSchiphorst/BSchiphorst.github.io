## PhD Research

He is finalizing a part-time PhD at the Korteweg-de Vries Institute (KdVI) for Mathematics at the University of Amsterdam, under the supervision of Prof. dr. Michel Mandjes, Prof. dr. Peter Spreij, and Prof. dr. ir. Erik Winands. This research position was financed by Rabobank and completed whilst employed there as a (quantitative) risk modeller.

The research focuses on the mathematical analysis of default/ruin/survival probabilities modelled through various stochastic processes, such as continuous-time Markov chains, multi-factor Merton models and (spectrally negative) Levy processes. A significant part focuses on model extensions that include network-based contagion effects, with the aim of preserving tractable computations through efficient numerical algorithms or closed-form results.
