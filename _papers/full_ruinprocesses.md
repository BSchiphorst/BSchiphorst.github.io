Abstract: 'This paper is currently still in the process of being written. 

    It analyses the ruin probabilities of risk reserve processes that are connected through a tree-like network structure. The root node of the network is assumed to follow a spectrally negative Levy process, while non-root nodes receive premia that depend on the risk reserve of their parent node. Upon the ruin of a root node, it is remove from the network and its child nodes become root nodes themselves. 

    We derive elegant closed-form solutions for the ruin probabilities for the special case of standard Cramér-Lundberg processes.
